Privacy Policy for Little Wild: Fox Adventure

Effective date: 6 November 2026

Little Wild: Fox Adventure, also called Little Wild (package name: com.littlewild.game), is provided by Aivyx Labs. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Android game.

Privacy contact: rizutechstudio@gmail.com

Want to delete your Little Wild account?

You can request deletion of your account and associated cloud progress without reinstalling the game. View account deletion instructions.

1. Information We Handle

Guest Progress and Settings

You can play without signing in. Guest progress, earned currency, character unlocks, achievements, medals and preferences are stored on your device. Local data may also be handled by your device's backup settings.

Guest play does not prevent the advertising, usage or diagnostic services described below from processing information.

Optional Google Sign-In and Cloud Saves

If you choose Google sign-in, Google and Firebase Authentication process account information such as your Google user identifier, Firebase user ID, email address, display name and profile information made available through sign-in.

We use this information to authenticate you, display your account identity and associate your cloud save with your account.

Cloud Firestore stores supported game progress, including trail completion, earned currency, character ownership, selected character, reward history, achievements and save timestamps or revision information. Some local features, including medals and device settings, may not be included in cloud saves.

We do not request access to your Gmail inbox, contacts or Google account password.

Usage Information

The Android release uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It processes app interactions, screen views, level starts and results, world and trail identifiers, practice-mode activity, collected-acorn counts, app and device information, and app-instance identifiers.

Google may also process automatically collected events, advertising identifiers where available, and location information derived from network information.

Our custom analytics events do not attach your email address, display name or Firebase account ID. This does not mean that analytics information is anonymous or that the SDK does not use other identifiers.

Crash Reports and Diagnostics

Firebase Crashlytics processes error messages, stack traces, relevant app state, app and operating-system versions, device characteristics, installation identifiers and diagnostic context such as the current screen, world or trail.

We use this information to find crashes and improve stability.

Rewarded Advertising

Optional rewarded ads are provided by Google AdMob. The advertising SDK may process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, advertising or app-set identifiers, device information, ad interactions, usage events and diagnostics to deliver and measure advertising and prevent fraud.

The current app requests non-personalized ads. Non-personalized ads still involve information processing and do not make the advertising service anonymous. Advertising availability and processing depend on applicable privacy requirements, SDK configuration and device settings.

Messages You Send Us

If you email us for support or privacy requests, we receive your email address, the contents of your message and any attachments you provide.

Please do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary sensitive information.

2. Why We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Operate the game and provide its features.
  • Remember progress and preferences.
  • Authenticate optional accounts.
  • Synchronize supported cloud saves.
  • Provide optional rewarded advertising.
  • Understand gameplay and app usage.
  • Diagnose errors and improve stability.
  • Prevent abuse and fraud.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

Where required, processing relies on an appropriate legal basis, such as providing a service you request, consent, legitimate interests or legal obligations. The applicable basis depends on the information, purpose and jurisdiction. A consent requirement must be satisfied before processing that requires it.

3. Providers and Disclosure

We use Google services, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics and Google AdMob.

These providers and their subprocessors receive information needed to provide their services. Their processing may also be governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

Aivyx Labs does not sell your account profile or cloud-save data. Advertising-related disclosures remain subject to the advertising provider's processing practices and applicable law.

We may disclose information when legally required or reasonably necessary to protect users, investigate abuse, enforce rights or maintain security.

If operation of the game transfers to another provider, relevant information may transfer with it subject to applicable safeguards and notice requirements.

4. Storage, Security and International Processing

Local saves are stored within the app's device storage. Cloud and SDK services use encrypted network connections. We use access controls for account-based cloud saves.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Google and its service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Applicable contractual and legal safeguards govern international processing.

5. Retention

Local progress and preferences remain until removed through device or app-storage controls, or replaced during normal use. Uninstalling or clearing app storage may remove local data. Device backups may retain or restore copies according to your backup settings.

Account records and active cloud saves are retained while your account exists, unless deletion is requested or a lawful retention requirement applies.

In-app account deletion removes the Firebase Authentication account and its active cloud-save record, as well as account-specific progress and conflict backups held by the app on that device.

Analytics information is retained according to the project's configured Google Analytics retention settings and Google's applicable processing rules.

Crashlytics generally retains crash information and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal from live and backup systems. AdMob information is retained according to Google's applicable service policies.

Support correspondence is kept while needed to handle the request and any related legal or security matters. Information subject to a legal retention obligation is kept for that obligation.

Account deletion does not automatically remove all independent SDK records, aggregate reports or provider backups. Provider retention and deletion processes apply.

6. Your Choices and Account Deletion

You can use guest play instead of optional Google sign-in and can decline to watch rewarded ads. Signing out does not delete an existing cloud account or disable diagnostic and usage collection.

Request Deletion Without Reinstalling the Game

To request deletion of your Little Wild: Fox Adventure account and associated cloud progress held by Aivyx Labs:

  1. Email rizutechstudio@gmail.com , preferably from the email address used for game sign-in.
  2. Use the subject Little Wild account deletion.
  3. State the email address used for game sign-in and that you want your game account and associated data deleted.

Email an Account Deletion Request

This button opens your email application. Send the email to submit your request. If it does not open, manually email the address above.

You do not need to reinstall the game to submit a request. We may request limited information to verify that you control the account. Never send your Google password.

We process verified requests without undue delay and within applicable legal requirements.

What Is Deleted

Account deletion removes your Little Wild Firebase Authentication account, including the account profile held for the game, and its active Cloud Firestore save. The cloud save includes supported trail completion, earned currency, character ownership, selected character, reward history, achievements and save timestamps or revision information.

Deletion does not delete your Google account. Your separate guest save remains available. For data held on your device, use the local-data controls described below.

Data That May Remain

Account deletion does not automatically remove all independent analytics, advertising or diagnostic SDK records, aggregate reports or provider backups. These follow the retention and deletion processes described in Section 5: Retention.

Crashlytics generally retains crash information and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal. Analytics and AdMob records follow the applicable project settings and provider policies. Support correspondence may be retained while needed to handle your request and related legal or security matters. Information subject to a legal retention obligation is kept for that obligation.

Delete Your Account in the Game

Open Settings → Account & cloud save, then use the account deletion option. You will be asked to verify your Google identity.

In-app deletion removes the game account and associated active cloud progress, as well as account-specific progress and conflict backups held by the app on that device. Your separate guest save remains available. It does not delete your Google account.

Local Data and Advertising Controls

Use Android's app-storage controls to remove guest progress and local settings. Advertising identifier and advertising privacy controls are available through your Android or Google settings. Available options vary by device.

Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal information, restrict or object to certain processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on it, or complain to a data protection authority.

Contact us to exercise applicable rights. We may need to verify your identity.
